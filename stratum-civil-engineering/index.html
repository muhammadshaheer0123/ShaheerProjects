  1. 01 / 04 — Investigate

    Read the ground first.

    Every structure starts below grade. Boreholes and cores tell us what the strata will do under load — before a single line is drawn.

    26 boreholes · 1.2 km of core · 3 strata
  2. 02 / 04 — Found

    Pylons rise from rock.

    Bored piles found each pylon in weathered chalk. The legs climb in 4 m slipform lifts and are tied by cross-beams at deck and crown.

    18 bored piles · Ø1.8 m · 2 pylons
  3. 03 / 04 — Span

    Balanced, module by module.

    Steel truss deck modules are lifted out from each pylon in pairs, so the structure stays in balance until the two cantilevers meet mid-span.

    44 steel truss modules · S460 · 6 m each
  4. 04 / 04 — Tension

    Tuned, then opened.

    Forty-eight stays are stressed to within 2% of design load. After the load test, the city drives across.

    48 stays · ±2% tuning · 100-yr design life

§ 01Studio140 engineers · 4 studios

We think the best infrastructure is the kind nobody notices — because it carries its load, quietly, for a hundred years.

Founded in Oslo in 1987 by two bridge engineers and a geologist, Stratum now works from four studios on structures that range from footbridges to kilometre-long viaducts.

We stay small enough that the engineer who signs the calculation is the one who visits site. Every project, whatever its size, gets a ground model, an independent check and a monitoring plan.

§ 04MethodFive stages, one team

Groundfirst.

Most structural problems start below grade. So does every Stratum project.

  1. 01

    Survey

    LiDAR, bathymetry and boreholes build a picture of the site as it actually is, not as the old maps say it was.

    25 m grid · 1,240 points / ha
  2. 02

    Model

    A live ground model, tested against every load case and every season of groundwater we can find records for.

    3 strata · 14 load cases
  3. 03

    Design

    Structure and foundation are designed by one team, together, rather than handed across a fence between firms.

    Raft footprint 68 × 40 m
  4. 04

    Build

    Our engineers stay on site through construction, and sensor data from the excavation is reviewed every morning.

    Formation −10.0 m · daily review
  5. 05

    Monitor

    Strain gauges, piezometers and settlement markers keep reporting for decades after handover. We keep reading them.

    212 sensors · live since 2009
